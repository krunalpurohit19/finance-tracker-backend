Delete your account

This permanently closes your Finance Tracker account. You can also do this in the app: More → Settings → Delete account.

  • Your sign-in stops working immediately on every device.
  • Your accounts, transactions, budgets, goals and settings are erased from our systems within 30 days.
  • Backups containing your data expire within a further 30 days.

Forgot your password? Reset it in the app first (Sign in → Forgot password).
